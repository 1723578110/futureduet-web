01 · Insight

One policy, two predictive roles

Main and wrist cameras complement each other at control time, but their reference frames expose different prediction problems.

Stable scene frame

Main view → Task State

Future RGB follows task evolution across the scene. Interaction masks and robot skeletons focus supervision on task objects and robot motion.

  • Scene-level progress
  • Task-object structure
  • Articulated robot motion
+

Moving camera frame

Wrist views → Interaction State

Future latent prediction follows short-horizon interaction changes without requiring pixel reconstruction of rapid viewpoint shifts and self-occlusion.

  • Relative pose
  • Contact evolution
  • Local object response

Design principle. Keep complementary observations together for action generation; design their future supervision separately.

02 · Method

Task evolution meets interaction evolution

FutureDuet trains two visual streams with view-specific future objectives. A Duet Interface brings their compact states back together for ActionDiT.

1

Shape each state

Structured main-view futures emphasize scene-level task evidence; wrist future targets supervise local temporal structure.

2

Read both states

ActionDiT jointly reads Task State and Interaction State so scene progress and close-range evidence can guide the same action chunk.

3

Deploy without predictors

The dense heads, V-JEPA teacher, and wrist predictor are removed after training.

Wrist pathway study

Separation helps; temporal supervision helps further

A lightweight separate wrist route reaches 60.9% without future supervision. Holding that deployed route fixed, future RGB reaches 64.1% and future latent supervision reaches 69.7%. All three variants share the same 201.20 ms policy-call latency.

Where difficulty remains

Reaching the object is not the same as completing the interaction

Open Microwave, Hanging Mug, and Turn Switch expose failures after the policy arrives at the relevant region: insufficient opening, a missed hook, or misaligned contact.

04 · Readout evidence

Structured targets shape what actions read

Mask and skeleton supervision produce different, interpretable shifts in action readout rather than merely adding more losses.
